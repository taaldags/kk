## How to find people fast

On LinkedIn people search, try these in order:

1. `Soka University` + company name (alumni are the easiest yes)
2. `Mongolia` or `Mongolian` + company name (shared background gets a lot of replies)
3. Company + team name + `analyst`, filter by "2nd degree"
4. Company + `university recruiter` or `campus recruiting` or `early talent`
5. Past interns: company + `intern` + `2026`

Aim for 2 people per company: one person on the team and one recruiter. Analysts 1 to 3 years in reply way more than VPs.

## Messages

Keep them short. People reply to short.

### LinkedIn connection note (under 300 characters)

> Hi [Name], I'm Nate, an Econ and Math student at Soka University. I just applied to the [role] at [Company] and would love to hear how you got into [team]. Open to a quick 15 min chat sometime?

### Alumni version

> Hi [Name], I'm Nate, a fellow Soka student (Econ and Math). I saw you're at [Company] on the [team] side and I just applied to their [role]. Would you be up for a 15 min call? I'd really like to hear what the work is actually like.

### Recruiter message (after applying)

> Hi [Name], I applied to the [role] (req [ID]) on [date]. I'm an Econ and Math double major at Soka University and I'm really interested in [specific thing about the team]. I'm an international student on F-1, so I wanted to ask if this role is open to candidates who'll need sponsorship later. Thanks either way!

Asking about sponsorship upfront saves me weeks of waiting on a role that was never possible.

### Follow-up (if no reply after 7 to 10 days)

> Hi [Name], just bumping this in case it got buried. Totally understand if you're busy. Thanks!

Only follow up once. After that, move on to the next person.

### Thank you (same day as the call)

> Thanks again for taking the time today, [Name]. Your point about [something specific they said] was really helpful. I'll keep you posted on how the [role] app goes.

### When the call goes well

At the end of the call, ask: "Is there anyone else on the team you think I should talk to?" Or: "Would you be comfortable passing my resume along?" Don't ask for a referral in the first message.

## Coffee chat questions (pick 3 or 4)

- How did you end up on this team?
- What does a normal week look like for an intern or first-year analyst?
- What made the strongest interns stand out?
- Does the team usually hire interns who need sponsorship later?
- Anything you wish you knew before applying?
